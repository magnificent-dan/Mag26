01 — About

A designer for the business that hasn't had one yet.

I design brand identities, product collateral, and marketing materials for small businesses — the kind with a great product and no one who's ever given their branding real attention. My work spans logos, packaging, price lists, social kits, and print materials, built to look consistent everywhere a customer sees the brand.

I bring a structured, research-driven approach to every brief — from first concept to final files — so what you get isn't just a nice graphic, but a system you can keep using.
